Oral Care Precision

Designed for optimal oral care, our toothbrush bristle filaments deliver the ideal balance of flexibility, recovery, and durability required by modern toothbrush manufacturers.

  • High bend recovery
  • Consistent diameter
  • Excellent tufting performance

Artistic Expression

Our artist brush filaments simulate the best characteristics of natural hair, offering excellent color holding capacity, smooth flow, and a precise, lasting point for fine detail work.

  • Fine tapering
  • Shape retention
  • Smooth medium application

Cosmetic Perfection

Cosmetics demand the finest touch. Our eyeliner brush filaments are drawn and finished to ultra-fine tolerances, ensuring a soft feel combined with rigid control for precise makeup application.

  • Ultra-soft tips
  • Chemical resistance
  • Firm root structure

About SAMAR FILAMENT

Dedicated to Brush Manufacturing Excellence

Located in Surat, Gujarat, SAMAR FILAMENT is established with a singular focus: providing high-quality bristle and filament solutions to the brush manufacturing industry.

We recognize that the performance of a toothbrush, the stroke of an artist's brush, and the precision of an eyeliner applicator all depend heavily on the physical properties of the filament used. That is why we dedicate our resources strictly to these three primary applications.

Our state-of-the-art facility at Ekta Industrial Estate ensures consistent production aligned with our core values of Trust, Value, and Respect.
