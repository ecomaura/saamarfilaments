Buying a New Car? Inspect It Before You Accept It.

Even new vehicles can leave the showroom with issues. A pre-delivery inspection gives you a clear picture before you sign off.

Why Inspect a New Car?

A new car isn't automatically free of issues — transport damage, storage wear, or manufacturing inconsistencies can go unnoticed at delivery. Our New Car PDI checks the vehicle across exterior, interior, mechanical and documentation categories before you accept it.
